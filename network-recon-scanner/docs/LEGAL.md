# Legal and ethical notice

Scanning systems you do not own or have no written permission to test is illegal in many countries
(e.g. Computer Fraud and Abuse Act in the US, Article 323-1 of the Code pénal in France, Law 07-03 in Morocco).

Rules for this project:

1. Only scan your own machines, a lab, or targets that explicitly allow it (`scanme.nmap.org`).
2. Keep `ALLOW_PUBLIC` disabled unless you hold written authorisation.
3. The author accepts no responsibility for misuse.
